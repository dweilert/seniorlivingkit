How We Help

We meet you exactly where you are in your journey.

At Senior Living Kit, we offer two distinct types of support - placement services and consulting services - to meet you exactly where you are in your journey.

Placement Services - FREE

If you or your loved one is ready to explore senior living communities, we offer hands-on placement support at no cost to families. We're compensated by the community you choose, so you can receive expert guidance without the added financial burden.

Consulting Services - $150/hour

Not ready to move - or need support beyond placement? We offer paid consulting services to guide you through other complex areas of senior care.

Central Texas senior living support.

We work primarily in Central Texas - Georgetown, Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, and San Antonio.

Not sure which path is right for you? Let's talk. We'll help you understand your options and build a plan that works for your family.
