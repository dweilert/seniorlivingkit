A roadmap designed for you

Support for the full senior care journey.

Senior Living Kit offers placement services and paid consulting so families can choose the level of support that fits their needs.

Placement Services - Free

For families ready to explore senior living communities, placement support is available at no cost to the family. Senior Living Kit is compensated by the community selected.

  • Assisted living, independent living, memory care, and continuing-care guidance
  • Curated recommendations across Georgetown, Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, and San Antonio
  • Tour preparation, pricing review, and decision support

Consulting Services - $150/hour

Paid consulting helps families who are not ready to move or need support beyond placement.

  • Downsizing and transition planning
  • Medicare, Medicaid, VA benefits, long-term-care insurance, and care navigation
  • Care-management and family-support planning
