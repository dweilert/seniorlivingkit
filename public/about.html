Our story

Your life still deserves new beginnings.

Choosing senior living is practical and emotional. Senior Living Kit exists to take stress out of the process through expert guidance and a trusted network.

Mission

Empower seniors and their families with the knowledge and resources they need to make confident, informed decisions about their next chapter.

Vision

A world where aging is met with dignity, clarity, and ease, and families feel supported every step of the way.

Founder

Kit Baumann founded Senior Living Kit after a lifetime of caregiving and nearly 14 years in the senior living industry. The service is designed to provide the hands-on, concierge-style support families need when making difficult care decisions.
